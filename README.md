# Approach & Focus
I build lean, resilient systems and media tools that favor **speed, security, and privacy**. Stack-agnostic and community-minded, I choose the simplest solution that scales, keep it portable (legacy → modern), and design for clear, consistent UX.

## Principles
- **Performance-first, minimal dependencies** — ship fast, stay flexible.
- **Security & privacy by default** — data minimization, no tracking.
- **Modular & portable** — small pieces, easy to extend and move.
- **Human-centered UX** — predictable interactions across touch/desktop.
- **Pragmatic & community-driven** — reuse, document, and contribute back.
